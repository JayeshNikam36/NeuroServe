# NeuroServe v0.1.0 — streaming OpenAI-compatible server with pluggable scheduling

The end of Phase 2. NeuroServe now serves many requests at once on one GPU, streams them, and
says why it scheduled each one the way it did.

## Highlights

- **OpenAI-compatible streaming.** `/v1/chat/completions` and `/v1/completions` with
  `"stream": true` (server-sent events in OpenAI's chunk format; the official `openai` client
  works). Output is Hugging Face-exact, streaming included.
- **Continuous batching.** It runs on a dedicated C++ engine thread: batched decode, a per-step
  token budget, KV pages allocated on demand, and preemption by recompute.
- **Four scheduling policies:** `fcfs`, `token-budget`, `decode-first` and `priority` with aging,
  switchable at runtime (`PUT /v1/runtime/policy`).
- **Explainable decisions.** `GET /v1/runtime/decisions` says why each request was admitted,
  deferred, rejected or preempted, with the numbers.
- **Production behaviour:**
  - cancellation, by `DELETE /v1/requests/{id}` or a client disconnect;
  - a bounded queue (429) and a queue timeout (503);
  - OpenAI-format errors;
  - JSON logs with request ids.
- **Latency metrics.** Queue time, TTFT, ITL and e2e for every request, and percentiles in
  `/health`.
- **Benchmarking tools:**
  - a seeded open-loop load generator;
  - a policy comparison with paired, order-balanced rounds;
  - NVML GPU sampling.

## First results (RTX 4050 Laptop GPU, Llama 3.2 1B FP16)

| Profile | Policy | TTFT P50 | TTFT P95 | ITL P50 | max ITL P95 | e2e P95 | output tok/s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `short` | `fcfs` | 29.8 ms | 149 ms | 16.5 ms | 49.9 ms | 1.25 s | 234 |
| `mixed` | `fcfs` | 51.9 ms | 888 ms | 16.8 ms | 147 ms | 6.75 s | 255 |
| `mixed` | `decode-first` | 22.3 s | 29.5 s | 16.1 ms | 82.7 ms | 32.8 s | 180 |

These are medians over 6 measured rounds of 120 requests each.
- On `short` the three policies are within ±2.1% of each other, and on `mixed` `token-budget`
  matches `fcfs`.
- `decode-first` cuts the worst gaps between tokens by 44%, but without chunked prefill it starves
  long prompts at 2 requests/s: 35 of 720 requests timed out.

Full report: [benchmarks/results/day24-policy-comparison.md](https://github.com/JayeshNikam36/NeuroServe/blob/v0.1.0/benchmarks/results/day24-policy-comparison.md).

## Verification

`scripts/release_check.sh` passed from a fresh clone. It ran every test (CPU, GPU, integration,
Python) and then exercised the live server through the official openai client: under every policy,
24 concurrent streams each matched the same request sent alone.
